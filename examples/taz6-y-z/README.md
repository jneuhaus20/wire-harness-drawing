# TAZ 6 Y-Z harness

The Y-Z motor harness of the LulzBot TAZ 6 3D printer, redrawn from LulzBot drawing EL-HR0091 rev A, "TAZ6 Y-Z Harness", © 2016 Aleph Objects, Inc., licensed CC BY-SA 4.0 and published at <https://download.lulzbot.com/TAZ/6.0/production_parts/electronics/Wire_Harnesses/External_Cable_Harnesses/>.

The parts, pinout, cable lengths and strip lengths are LulzBot's. Their drawing is in [`lulzbot/`](lulzbot/), with drawing EL-HR0143, whose parts list gives the supplier part behind each LulzBot part number; [`tests/test_lulzbot_example.py`](../../tests/test_lulzbot_example.py) reads both and checks the generated package against them. The route positions are estimated from LulzBot's TAZ 6 assembly instructions and frame parts list; see the comments in `taz6-y-z.yaml`.
